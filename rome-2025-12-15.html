Top Attractions

  • Colosseum – This ancient amphitheater is one of Rome's most iconic landmarks and a testament to the city's rich history.
  • Vatican City – The center of the Catholic Church, Vatican City is home to numerous iconic landmarks, including St. Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel.
  • Roman Forum – Once the heart of ancient Rome, the Roman Forum features a collection of ancient ruins, including temples, basilicas, and markets.
  • Pantheon – This ancient temple is one of the best-preserved ancient buildings in Rome and a masterpiece of Roman architecture.
  • Trevi Fountain – This beautiful baroque fountain is a popular spot for tourists and locals alike, and legend has it that if you throw a coin into the fountain, you'll one day return to Rome.
