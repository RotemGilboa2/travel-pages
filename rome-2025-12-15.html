Top Attractions

  • Colosseum – This ancient amphitheater is one of Rome's most iconic landmarks and a testament to the city's rich history.
  • Vatican City – The Vatican is home to numerous iconic landmarks, including St. Peter's Basilica, the Sistine Chapel, and the Vatican Museums.
  • Trevi Fountain – This beautiful baroque fountain is a must-see attraction in Rome, where visitors can make a wish by tossing a coin into the water.
  • Roman Forum – Once the heart of ancient Rome, the Roman Forum is a vast archaeological site filled with ancient ruins and historic landmarks.
  • Pantheon – This magnificently preserved ancient temple is one of Rome's most impressive architectural achievements.
  • Castel Sant'Angelo – This former fortress and mausoleum offers stunning views of the city and is a great spot to take in the sights.
