Top Attractions

  • Colosseum – This ancient amphitheater is one of Rome's most iconic landmarks and a testament to the city's rich history.
  • Vatican City – The Vatican is home to numerous iconic landmarks, including St. Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel, featuring Michelangelo's famous ceiling.
  • Trevi Fountain – This beautiful baroque fountain is a must-see for its stunning architecture and picturesque surroundings.
  • Spanish Steps – This grand staircase is one of Rome's most famous landmarks and offers stunning views of the city.
  • Pantheon – This ancient temple is one of Rome's best-preserved ancient buildings and features an impressive dome designed by Michelangelo.
