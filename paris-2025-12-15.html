Top Attractions

  • The Eiffel Tower – The iconic symbol of Paris and one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world.
  • The Louvre Museum – One of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing an impressive collection of art and artifacts.
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral – A beautiful and historic Gothic church that has been a Parisian landmark for over 850 years.
  • Arc de Triomphe – A monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France, offering stunning views of the city.
  • Montmartre – A charming and historic neighborhood known for its bohemian vibe, street artists, and stunning views of the city.
