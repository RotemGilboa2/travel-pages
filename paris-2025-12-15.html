Top Attractions

  • The Eiffel Tower – The iconic iron lattice tower built for the 1889 World's Fair, offering breathtaking views of the city.
  • The Louvre Museum – One of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing an impressive collection of art and artifacts from around the world, including the Mona Lisa.
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral – A beautiful and historic Gothic cathedral that has been a symbol of Paris for over 800 years, featuring stunning stained glass windows and intricate stone carvings.
  • Arc de Triomphe – A monumental arch honoring the soldiers who fought and died for France, offering panoramic views of the city from its rooftop.
  • Montmartre – A charming and bohemian neighborhood known for its narrow streets, artist studios, and stunning views of the city.
