Paris Travel Guide

Discover the City of Light, famous for its stunning architecture, art museums, fashion, and romantic atmosphere.

Top Attractions

  • The Eiffel Tower – Built for the 1889 World's Fair, this iconic iron lattice tower is a symbol of Paris and offers breathtaking views of the city.
  • The Louvre Museum – One of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing an impressive collection of art and artifacts from ancient civilizations to the 19th century.
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral – A beautiful and historic Gothic church that was built in the 12th century and has been a place of worship and pilgrimage for centuries.
  • Arc de Triomphe – A monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France, offering stunning views of the city from its rooftop.
  • Montmartre – A charming and historic neighborhood known for its bohemian vibe, street artists, and stunning views of the city from the top of the hill.
